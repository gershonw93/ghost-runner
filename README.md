# Ghost Runner

Your pacer. Always out front.

Pick a distance and a goal time, then run against a 3D ghost who holds that pace on your camera view. GPS tracks your distance, voice call-outs tell you how far ahead or behind you are, and every run is saved on your phone.

## Use it on iPhone

1. Open the site link in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Start** and allow Location, Camera and Motion.

Keep the screen on while you run. Runs, PRs and settings are stored only on your own phone.
